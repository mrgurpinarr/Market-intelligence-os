# Bitcoin Macro Monetary Correlation & Global Liquidity Index

## 1. Global M2 Aggregate Money Supply Velocity
Bitcoin functions as a pure monetary barometer responding to central bank balance sheet expansion:
- **Global Liquidity Index (GLI):** 89% positive 90-day correlation with global M2 liquidity aggregate expansion.
- **US Real Yield Inversion:** Declining 10-year TIPS real yields consistently trigger accelerated institutional capital rotations into non-sovereign stores of value.
- **Currency Devaluation Hedge:** Pronounced outperformance during emerging market currency stress regimes and domestic debt refinancing cycles.

## 2. Risk Factors & Downside Scenarios
- Extended restrictive monetary policy (higher-for-longer policy rates above neutral rate).
- Concentrated custody counterparty risks among institutional ETF custodians.
- Global regulatory interventions regarding unhosted non-custodial wallets.
